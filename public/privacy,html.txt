  <h1>Privacy Policy</h1>
  <p>Last updated: September 2026</p>

  <p>This Privacy Policy explains what information Signal ("the App", "we", "us")
  collects when you connect an Instagram account, and how that information is used.</p>

  <h2>Information We Collect</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>Your Instagram professional account's username and Instagram-scoped user ID, obtained when you connect your account via Instagram login.</li>
    <li>An access token issued by Meta, used solely to act on your behalf (sending replies, direct messages, and moderating comments) as configured by you.</li>
    <li>Content of comments and direct messages sent to your connected account, so that automations you create can be matched and triggered.</li>
    <li>Basic account information you provide when creating a Signal account, such as your email address and name.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>How We Use Information</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>To operate the automations you configure, including replying to comments, sending direct messages, and moderating incoming comments.</li>
    <li>To detect and flag messages that may relate to brand partnership inquiries, for your own review.</li>
    <li>To process digital product sales you set up, and deliver purchased files to buyers via direct message.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>What We Do Not Do</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>We do not sell your data or your Instagram audience's data to third parties.</li>
    <li>We do not use your data to serve advertising.</li>
    <li>We do not access your Instagram account for any purpose beyond the automations you have explicitly configured.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Data Storage &amp; Security</h2>
  <p>Data is stored in a managed database with access restricted to the application server.
  Access tokens are used only to make the specific API calls your automations require.</p>

  <h2>Data Deletion</h2>
  <p>You may disconnect your Instagram account at any time from your dashboard, which stops
  all further use of your access token. To request full deletion of your account data,
  contact us using the email below.</p>
